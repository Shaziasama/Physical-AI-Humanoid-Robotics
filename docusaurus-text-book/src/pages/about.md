# About This Book

This is an interactive textbook designed for those passionate about the future of Physical AI and humanoid robotics. 

Our goal is to provide a comprehensive, hands-on guide to building and programming intelligent robots that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world.

## About the Author
shazia Zohaib is an aspiring AI and robotics developer with a background in 
web technologies and applied artificial intelligence.

She is currently studying Artificial Intelligence and Web 3.0 at GIAIC and 
actively works on projects involving humanoid robotics, simulation, and 
LLM-powered cognitive systems. This book is designed to bridge the gap between 
theoretical concepts and real-world implementation in modern Physical AI systems.
